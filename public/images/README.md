## 🔄 How to Add Your Images

1. **Save your images in the appropriate folders**
2. **Open** `lib/images.ts`
3. **Comment out** the UNSPLASH_IMAGES section (lines 10-30)
4. **Uncomment** the LOCAL_IMAGES section (lines 32-52)
5. **Save and refresh** your browser

That's it! Your images will now be used instead of Unsplash.
